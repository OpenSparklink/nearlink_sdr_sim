nearlink_sdr.phy.measurement¶

Positioning measurement signal — TXS-10002-2025 standard 6.2.4

Implements signal generation required for SLE positioning measurement:

  • Narrowband waveform measurement signal 1 (6.2.4.1)

  • 窄带波形测量signal 2 (6.2.4.2)

  • 多天线测量signal天线对排序 (6.2.4.3)

generate测量signal 1 的比特sequence (6.2.4.1)。

无相位旋转 BPSK modulation, 返回比特sequence。

parameter: n_measur: 测量sequencelength [16, 32, 64, 128, 256, 512, 1024, 2048] security_type: security类型 (1~4) seed: security随机种子 (16 字节) slot_number: 起始调度time slot号 is_tx: True=先发节点, False=后发节点 n_disturb_max: maximum扰动符号数configuration [0-11] kdf_type: 0=AES-CMAC, 1=HMAC-SM3

返回: np.ndarray, dtype=int, length n_measur

generate测量signal 2 的复数基带波形 (6.2.4.2)。
