nearlink_sdr.phy.pluto_backend¶

PlutoSDR / ANTSDR backend – based on pyadi-iio.

Supports MicroPhase ANTSDR E310, Analog Devices ADALM-Pluto, and other AD9361 + libiio based SDR devices.
