nearlink_sdr.phy.rf_compliance¶

RF compliance parameters and verification — standard chapter 8.

Implements narrowband/UWB RF metrics defined in TXS-10002-2025 standard sections 8.2-8.4:

  • 8.2.1 Output power level

  • 8.2.2 Modulation accuracy (GFSK deviation / PSK EVM / frequency tolerance / clock accuracy)

  • 8.2.3 Unwanted emissions (GFSK/PSK in-band spurious)

  • 8.3.1 Receiver sensitivity and maximum input level

  • 8.3.2 Receiver selectivity

  • 8.3.4 RSSI Accuracy

  • 8.4 UWB RF (channel, spectral mask, frequency tolerance, RMS error)
