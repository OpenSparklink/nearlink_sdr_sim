Completed Extended Features¶

The following features go beyond the standard specification to enhance simulation system completeness:

  • Multi-user interference simulation (SIR modeling, co-channel/adjacent-channel): phy.channel + sim.link_sim

  • Doppler time-varying channel (Jakes sum-of-sinusoids model): phy.channel
